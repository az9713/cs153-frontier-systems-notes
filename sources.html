Sources, coverage, and research

Snapshot: 25 September 2026. This page records source provenance and section-level navigation. It is not a claim-level attribution ledger or a percentage measure of lecture-derived prose.

Method and limits

The supplied playlist was enumerated, every available caption source was read, and lecture arguments were reconstructed in independent prose. Mathematical developments, hypothetical numbers, and exercises are labeled as educational extensions. Primary papers and institutional documents support research additions and consequential corrections. Speaker opinions, predictions, and private commercial figures remain attributed claims.

Selected corrections include five-nines downtime (chapter 4), network pair counts (8), supplier nationality versus operating enrichment capacity (7), the Sullivan appellate outcome (3), and the scope of historical export controls and the CLOUD Act (8 and 13). These checks do not amount to verifying every biographical or commercial statement in the recordings.

Raw caption caches are local research inputs. The machine-readable manifest retains recording identifiers, source origins, quality notes, and SHA-256 hashes. The section map records each timestamped main heading. No raw captions are embedded in the reading pages.
